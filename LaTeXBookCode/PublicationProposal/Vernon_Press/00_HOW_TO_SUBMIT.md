# Vernon Press — how to submit

**Route:** email. There is no web form and no portal.
**Send to:** `submissions@vernonpress.com`
**Their pages:** [Publish with us](https://vernonpress.com/publish-with-us) · [Submitting a book proposal](https://vernonpress.com/content/3/92e0e48529cc3bb4e319030a09dad1b3)

## What to attach

| # | File | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **`Booth_Teas_VernonPress_proposal_FILLED.docx`** | **Ready to send.** Their nine numbered fields, already filled in. Opens in Pages, Google Docs, or LibreOffice; you do not need Word. One field is still blank, marked `[TO COMPLETE]`: field 9, how you heard about them. A `.pdf` twin sits beside it for proofreading or if you would rather send PDF. | 2 | One sample chapter (PDF) | See recommendation below. |
| 3 | Table of contents | Optional but they say it helps. Export pages iii–vi of `main.pdf`. |

Put the cover letter (`01_cover_letter.md`) in the **body of the email**, not as an attachment. Subject line:

> Book proposal: *Applied Program Evaluation Using Stata* (complete draft, 328pp)

## Do not send the full manuscript

Their guidance page says this outright: *"Please do not forward complete manuscripts as part of a proposal."* Send one chapter. If they want the rest they will ask, and that is when the peer review starts.

## Which sample chapter

**Send Chapter 13, "Using AI without getting burned."** It is the chapter a non-Stata editor can judge without knowing Stata, it is the most obviously current, and it is the one with no competing title anywhere. Chapter 2 ("Setting up a project that survives deadlines") is the alternate if you would rather lead with the workflow spine.

## What happens next, and when

Their own stated numbers:

- **Acknowledgement:** within 1 week of receipt.
- **Decision on the proposal:** about 2 weeks.
- They commit to replying to every complete proposal, with feedback where they can.
- If they are interested they will ask for the full manuscript, then send it to **at least two** peer reviewers.
- **Post-acceptance:** 6–8 months from full manuscript to publication; about 4 months is their stated fastest. Their FAQ breaks that into peer review ~2 months, author revisions 2–3 months, production ~7 weeks.

## Cost

None. They state it in three separate places, including *"We have a policy of never charging authors for publication"* and *"We undertake to publish your work at our own cost."* The only money item on their site is an optional Gold open-access add-on, which they explicitly decouple from the accept/reject decision. **You are not obliged to buy it and it does not affect whether they take the book.**

## Two things to decide before you send

1. **Field 8 asks about their Spanish-translation initiative (BRILS).** I drafted a measured yes. Read it and cut it if you would rather not open that door, because funding the translation would fall to you.
2. **Field 9 asks where you heard about them.** I left it blank on purpose; answer it honestly in a line.

## Honest read on fit

Vernon clears every one of your hard constraints: free, small, fast, no prestige gatekeeping, and they say in writing that they welcome first-time authors. The weakness is shelf fit. Their Statistics and Quantitative Methods list is one of their two smallest at about 23 titles, they have published nothing in Stata and nothing in program evaluation, and their nearest software precedent (*An Introduction to Computational Macroeconomics*, which ships Excel and MATLAB companion files) sits in economics methodology rather than applied social research. The cover letter and field 6 are written to carry that weight. They argue the readership directly, because there is no competing title on their list to argue against.
